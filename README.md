# The Mentalist

The Mentalist is one of my favorite shows. It ran from 2008 to 2015 on CBS and stars Simon Baker as Patrick Jane.

Jane used to pretend he was a psychic, but after a serial killer named Red John murdered his wife and daughter, he started working with the California Bureau of Investigation (CBI). He doesn’t actually have powers — he’s just really good at reading people and noticing tiny details that everyone else misses.

### Main characters
- Patrick Jane (Simon Baker)
- Teresa Lisbon (Robin Tunney)
- Kimball Cho (Tim Kang)
- Wayne Rigsby (Owain Yeoman)
- Grace Van Pelt (Amanda Righetti)

What I like most about the show is how Jane messes with suspects. He’ll say something random or act weird just to get a reaction out of them. The whole Red John storyline is also really good, especially toward the later seasons.

Overall it’s a solid crime show with good characters and some funny moments mixed in with the serious stuff.
